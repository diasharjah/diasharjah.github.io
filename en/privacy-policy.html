Privacy Policy - Sharjah Department of Islamic Affairs

The Sharjah Department of Islamic Affairs is committed to protecting the privacy of its website users. Please read the following privacy policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Collection of Personal Information

  • The Department does not automatically collect any personal information about users while browsing the website
  • Personal information is only collected when voluntarily submitted by users through electronic forms
  • Required information is limited to what is necessary for providing the requested service
  • By using the website, you agree to the terms and conditions of this privacy policy

Protection and Confidentiality of Information

  • The Department implements strict security measures to protect personal information
  • Access to information is restricted to authorized employees only and on a need-to-know basis
  • Personal information is not shared with any external parties without explicit user consent
  • The Department adheres to local and international data protection standards and regulations

Use of Electronic Forms

  • When submitting inquiries or requests through electronic forms, the provided information will be used only for the specified purpose
  • Required information may include: name, email, phone number, and any other information necessary for service delivery
  • Information will be retained only for the duration necessary to complete the requested service

Children's Protection

  • The Department pays special attention to protecting children's privacy online
  • A special section for children has been designed with consideration for their needs and privacy
  • We encourage parents to monitor and guide their children's online activities
  • No personal information is collected from children without parental consent

Cookies

  • The website may use cookies to enhance user experience
  • Users can control cookie settings through their internet browser
  • These files are not used to collect personal information

Privacy Policy Updates

  • The Department reserves the right to update or modify the privacy policy at any time
  • Changes will be posted on this page with the latest update date
  • Continued use of the website after posting modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
  • We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed of any updates
